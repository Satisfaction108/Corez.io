First 30 seconds (solo player, fresh spawn)
Land with impact. Drop-in from the sky with a dust ring and a soft thud — spawning should feel like arriving, not like a light switching on.
A rock in your face. Guarantee 2–3 drillable rocks within one screen of every spawn, and make the first rock you touch unusually weak — it cracks in ~2 seconds and showers gems. First crack fast = the player instantly "gets" the game.
Gem magnet + pitch ladder. Gems fly to you (they mostly do), but add a rising musical pitch per pickup in a chain — mining starts to sound like a slot machine paying out. Addictive, nearly free.
Combo meter for mining. Chaining rock cracks without pausing builds a ×2…×5 crack-speed or dust-bonus combo with a little meter under the tank. Suddenly even solo mining is a rhythm game.
A visible promise. Always have a loot chest visible (not reachable, visible) from spawn, shimmering. Curiosity is the cheapest motivator in games — the player walks toward it and learns movement, camera, and drilling on the way.
No dry starts, ever. Copper guaranteed near spawn; pity logic so a new player can never whiff the first 30s. Nothing kills a game faster than drilling three rocks and getting nothing.
First quest in 5 seconds. A small toast, not a popup: "Bank 100 dust → keep it forever." One line, a progress number, a reward. Purpose within one glance.
Core loop tension (mine → carry → risk → bank)
Carriers glow. The more you carry, the more you shimmer — predators can spot a fat satchel across the map. Greed becomes visible, and every player feels rich and hunted at the same time.
The walk back is the game. Banks should be just far enough that the return trip itches. Add shortcuts with costs (a launch pad that's loud and visible, a shortcut through a narrow rock canyon) so routing is a decision, not a commute.
Near-miss feedback. When you bank with <10% HP or with the storm a second behind you, say so: "Banked by a heartbeat!" Players remember close calls more than wins.
Fights worth having (not random deathmatch)
Everything valuable is loud. Chests, blooms, boss fights all ping the minimap. Fights should erupt over things, with both sides knowing the other is coming — that's drama instead of ambush.
Contested windows, not camping. Banks announce "contested" already — lean in: a capture bar both sides can see, so breaking a bank becomes a timed fight rather than backdooring an empty pad.
Bounties + revenge (from before) — the two cheapest drama engines: everyone hunts #1, and everyone gets a personal villain per life.
Power fantasy
Temp overdrive pickups. A rare drop: 20s of double drill speed or a wider beam. Short, loud, fun — and it expires, so no balance damage.
Streaks with a cost. Kill streaks pay escalating bonus but put you on the minimap. Power and danger in one mechanic.
Boss = slot machine. The warden doesn't just die — it erupts, gems bouncing everywhere with magnet delay so you scramble to vacuum. The kill is the foreplay; the scramble is the fun.
Dead time & downtime (secretly important)
Dead betting. While waiting out the 15s tax, let dead players predict the raid winner from the standings for a small dust prize. Spectating goes from boring to invested instantly.
Kill recap, not just killer cam. One line on the death screen: "Outdrilled, outgunned — 412 dust lost, 900 kept." Loss stings less when the ledger is clear, and "kept" softens everything.
Queue toys. Final-storm queue should let you fly the camera around the map freely. A bored queued player is a lost player.
Variety (raid #50 must surprise)
Raid modifiers. Each raid rolls one twist shown on the banner: "Copper Rush," "Fast Storm," "Bounty Week," "Iron Rocks." One line of variety, endless water-cooler.
Random events mid-raid. Meteor shower seeds ore in the open; gem rain in the safe zone pulls cowards out of corners. Announced, visible, optional.
If I had to pick the three that do the most work: weak first rock + pitch-ladder pickups (first 30s feel), carriers glow (the whole risk loop in one visual), and dead betting (turns your worst moment into a game). Say the word if you want any of these specced out properly.
