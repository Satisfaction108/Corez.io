The idea

Corez.io is a 2D tank battle royale where the whole map is made of rock. You shoot the rock to dig out gems (copper, azurite, purple shards and emeralds). If you die, you drop most of the gems you're carrying, so take them to a vault and bank them. Banked gems are safe.

A raid lasts 21 minutes and you can join at any time. The storm closes in 3 times, so by the end everyone is fighting in the same area. Whoever has the most points when the raid ends wins.

Your first minutes

  • Mine. Shoot the rock and drive over the gems that fall out.
  • Bank. Follow the VAULT arrow and drive onto the vault to bank your gems.
  • Level up. Put points into your stats with the number keys, and pick a new tank from the top left.
  • Shop. Shop pads sell drills, gear and kit items like the Rock Barrage. They only take banked gems.
  • Fight. Kill other tanks to take their gems. If you get revenge on whoever killed you, you get double the bounty.
  • Stay out of the storm. It does a lot of damage, so keep inside the circle as it closes.

New to the game? Press Tutorial on the menu. It takes about 10 minutes.

Modes

  • Solo: every tank for itself.
  • Duos: play with a friend (send them a party code) or get paired with a random player.

There are bots too, so a raid is never empty.

Accounts (optional)

You can play as a guest for as long as you like. If you log in with Discord or a username, your rank gets saved. Ranks go from Bronze up to Corez, and from Legend up you also get a number on the leaderboard. Accounts earn gemdust to spend on name styles and tank skins, and they come with friends and daily quests too.
