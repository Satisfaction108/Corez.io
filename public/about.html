The idea

Corez.io is a top-down tank battle royale set underground. You blast your way through solid rock to dig up copper, azurite, purple shards and emeralds. Gems you carry are up for grabs: if you get knocked out, you drop them. Drive them to a vault and bank them, and they are safe for the rest of the raid.

A raid lasts 21 minutes and you can join at any time. A storm closes in over three cycles, so sooner or later everyone ends up fighting over the same rock, and the last one standing wins.

Your first minutes

  • Mine. Shoot the rock to break it and scoop up the gems inside.
  • Bank. Follow the VAULT arrow at the edge of your screen and drive in to bank what you carry.
  • Level up. Spend skill points on stats (number keys) and pick a new tank from the tiles at the top left.
  • Shop. Spend banked gems at a shop pad on drills, gear and kit items like the Rock Barrage.
  • Fight. Knock out other tanks to take their haul. Take down the one who got you and you get double bounty.
  • Survive. Stay ahead of the storm. The top of the board at the end of the raid gets rewards.

New? Hit Tutorial on the main menu. It walks you through the basics in about ten minutes.

Modes

  • Solo: every tank for itself.
  • Duos: queue with a friend (share a party code) or get paired with someone.

Bots mine, bank and fight too, so a raid is never empty.

Accounts (optional)

You can play as a guest forever. Log in with Discord or a username to keep your rank (Bronze up to Emerald, then numbered Legend spots), earn gemdust for name styles and tank skins in the daily shop, add friends, and do daily quests.

Who made it

Corez.io is made by ZyroX. It is built on the open-source arras.io engine and the code is published under the AGPL. Come say hi, report bugs or suggest things on our Discord.
