The idea

Corez.io is a top down tank battle royale, but the whole map is rock. You shoot the rock to get gems out (copper, azurite, purple shards and emeralds). If you die you drop most of what you're carrying, so get it to a vault and bank it. Banked gems are safe.

A raid is 21 minutes and you can join any time. The storm closes in 3 times, so everyone ends up fighting in the same spot eventually. Most points at the end wins.

Your first minutes

  • Mine. Shoot rock, drive over the gems that fall out.
  • Bank. Follow the VAULT arrow and drive in.
  • Level up. Put points in stats with the number keys, and pick a new tank from the top left.
  • Shop. Shop pads take banked gems. Drills, gear, kit stuff like the Rock Barrage.
  • Fight. Kill other tanks and take their gems. Get back at whoever killed you for double bounty.
  • Stay out of the storm. It hurts a lot. Top of the board at the end gets paid.

New? Hit Tutorial on the menu. It's about 10 minutes.

Modes

  • Solo: every tank for itself.
  • Duos: play with a friend (send them a party code) or get paired with someone random.

There are bots too, so a raid is never empty.

Accounts (optional)

You can play as a guest forever, that's fine. If you log in (Discord or a username) your rank gets saved. Ranks go from Bronze up to Corez, and from Legend up you get an actual number. You also get gemdust to spend on name styles and tank skins, plus friends and daily quests.

Who made it

Me, ZyroX. It's built on the open source arras.io engine and the code is AGPL. Bugs, ideas, or just saying hi: Discord.
