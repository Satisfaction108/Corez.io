# Announcement [2026-09-24] We're Corez.io now
- New name, new logo (it's an octo-tank with an emerald in it, yes really).
- We have a <a href="https://discord.gg/qtwajWhaGc">Discord</a> now. Come say hi.

# Update [2026-09-24] Friends + chat
- Add people by name and see if they're in the menu, in a raid or off.
- Chat from the menu, or hit <b>Esc</b> mid raid for a quick chat.
- Unread counts, "seen", and a little popup when someone messages you.
- You can block people. They can't message you and you won't see them in game chat.

# Update [2026-09-24] Shop + locker
- You get <b>gemdust</b> for banking gems, knockouts and top 3 raid finishes.
- Shop changes every day at midnight UTC. 2 big items and 4 daily ones.
- 13 name styles (custom colour up to animated rainbow) and 12 tank skins.
- You can gift stuff to friends. And you get 3 refunds total if you regret a buy.
- Wear stuff from your locker. It shows up next time you spawn.

# Update [2026-09-24] Ranked
- Bronze up to Emerald, then Legend where you get an actual number.
- First 3 games are placements, they decide where you start.
- Every death shows how much rank you got, and ranking up gets a big screen.
- Rank badges next to names and on the leaderboard.
- Daily quests for extra gemdust, and 12 medals to collect.
- Top 100 leaderboard and profiles with your stats.

# Update [2026-09-24] Accounts
- Log in with Discord or a name + password. Or just keep playing as a guest, that's fine too.
- Your rank, gemdust and stuff are saved.
- You get a backup code when you sign up in case you forget your password.
- Rename every 14 days, link/unlink Discord, or delete your account in Account.
