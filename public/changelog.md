# Announcement [2026-09-24] Welcome to Corez.io
- Our battle royale has a name: <b>Corez.io</b>! Say hi to our shiny new emerald octo-tank logo.
- Come hang out with us on the new <a href="https://discord.gg/qtwajWhaGc">Discord server</a>.

# Update [2026-09-24] Friends and chat
- Add friends by their username and see who's in the menu, in a raid, or offline.
- Chat with your friends right from the menu, or press <b>Esc</b> mid-raid to open a quick chat.
- Unread badges, "Seen" receipts, and a little pop-up when someone messages you.
- Block anyone who's bugging you. They can't message you, and you won't see their chat in game.

# Update [2026-09-24] Item Shop and Locker
- Earn <b>gemdust</b> by banking gems, knocking out other players, and placing top 3 in a raid.
- The Item Shop refreshes every day at midnight UTC with 2 featured items and 4 daily picks.
- 13 name styles, from a simple custom colour to animated rainbow names, plus 12 tank skins.
- Gift items to your friends, and you get 3 refunds if you change your mind.
- Equip everything from the Locker. It shows up the next time you spawn.

# Update [2026-09-24] Ranked
- Climb from Bronze all the way to Emerald, then fight for a numbered spot in Legend.
- Your first 3 games are placement games that decide where you start.
- Every death shows how much rank you gained, and ranking up gets a big celebration screen.
- Rank badges now show up next to player names and on the leaderboard.
- New daily quests for bonus gemdust, plus 12 achievements to unlock.
- A Top 100 leaderboard and player profiles with your stats.

# Update [2026-09-24] Accounts
- Log in with Discord or a username and password. Or just keep playing as a guest!
- Your rank, gemdust and items are saved to your account.
- New accounts get a recovery code in case you ever forget your password.
- Change your username every 14 days, link or unlink Discord, or delete your account from Account settings.
