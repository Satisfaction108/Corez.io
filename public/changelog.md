# Update [2026-10-30] Arms Race tanks are here!
- 425 Arms Race tanks from arras.io joined the tank tree, and all the made-up tanks are gone. Stalker finally upgrades to Owl!
- Every tank was rebalanced after about 33,000 test duels, so each one should win around half its fights.
- Tanks and drones have the classic arras.io look, and drones now grow in size with your tank.
- The home page is cleaner: Raid Night on the left, Daily Rewards and Featured Tanks on the right, and a new card with today's standings and yesterday's winners. Bots count in the standings too.
- FFA growth now matches 2TDM, so you can grow up to 5x.
- The storm now hurts everywhere outside the circle, so no more hiding in the corners.
- Kills pay more the bigger the tank you take down, and you keep 25% of your score when you respawn.
- Perks, abilities and drill upgrades are toned down, and some perks and shop items were taken out.
- In 2TDM your team now votes on a Radar Sweep, a Supply Drop or a Titan when the War Meter fills, and the Titan has a new look.
- The smoke cloud is no longer pixelated.
- Phones get new Repel and Override buttons.

# Update [2026-10-22] 2TDM is here!
- <a href="#cinematic" class="dw-watch-cinematic">Watch cinematic</a>
- Blue vs Red! You get put on whichever team needs you, and bots fill both sides so there's always a full fight.
- Each team has a base on its side of the map. Your base is safe, but step on the enemy's tiles and you die on the spot, and their Base Protectors send drones at anyone who gets close.
- Bank gems to raise your team score. The Team Bank is in the middle of your base, the Eye Vault is a forward bank just outside it, and the Center Vault is neutral (and the most dangerous place to bank). The score resets every day.
- Banking and winning fights fill your team's War Meter. Spend it at the War Pad in your base on a Radar Sweep, two Supply Crates only your team can open, or a giant Titan that marches on the enemy base.
- Kill an enemy Titan for a team bounty and a huge pile of gems. Base Protectors only chip at a Titan, so someone has to go stop it.
- Take an Outpost out on the map to bank and sometimes respawn there.
- Events are team battles now, and the Core is the Mother Lode, a giant gem deposit in the middle. No Storm Circle in 2TDM.
- Invite a friend to your team with the new button next to fullscreen, and press G to drop an enemy marker your team can see.
- Hunted is now simply the #1 player on the leaderboard, in FFA too. In 2TDM each team hunts the other team's #1.
- Save codes only restore in the mode you saved them in.
- Spawn and vault shields now end once you actually drive off.
- Regrowing rocks are tougher and drop their gems when you break them, and breaking rocks hits a lot harder.
- Ranger no longer upgrades into Crosshair, Falcon-style tanks fire their main gun with right click, and Firefly is gone.
- Bots score properly, move smoothly, and come help you when you're getting shot.

# Update [2026-10-14] A more... brighter world
- The whole game has a new look: bright rocks with bold outlines, a camo floor under the grid instead of dirt, and a softer purple storm. Dark mode on the home page now makes the game dark too. And light mode makes it light. Obviously :)
- Tanks look like classic diep tanks, with brighter player colours, thicker outlines, and barrels that actually "kick" back when they fire. You no longer always spawn as blue.
- Every skin has been redrawn in the same clean cartoon style.
- The map has bigger open areas around the vaults and shops, joined by wide paths, so more fights happen out in the open. Mined rock grows back much faster now.
- Movement is smoother, with a little more weight when you start and stop.
- Chests and supply crates now drop an ability you can drive over to pick up. Chests and the supply crate also have new art.
- Drone and minion tanks are weaker, and their drones and minions mine much more slowly.
- Your spawn shield shows a countdown ring, and it tells you why it ended.
- The HUD is much quieter, and every screen and menu matches your light or dark theme. Level ups and other news now show up in a small stack on the right.
- The home page is simpler, with a live world behind it where bots mine through the rocks. New players just see their name, Play and the region, and everything else shows up after their first game.
- The game is now one world that is always on, with a new event every few minutes and a daily leaderboard. The same 10 bots stick around for the whole day.
- Storm Circle now closes in from the edge of the map over 3 minutes.
- King of the Vault always pays while you are inside it. If someone else is on the hill, everyone on it earns half.
- Reloading the page puts you right back where you were, without the drop animation.
- When you die, you now respawn with 60% of your banked gems instead of a small starter amount. Your points and carried gems work the same as before.
- Fixed the question mark tooltips not showing on Mac screens.
 - psst try out light mode :D
