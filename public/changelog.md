# Announcement [2026-09-24] We're Corez.io now
- The game has a new name and a new logo. The logo is an octo-tank with an emerald in it, yes really.
- We have a <a href="https://discord.gg/bx5FK3CMwp">Discord</a> now, so come and say hi!

# Update [2026-09-24] Friends and chat
- You can add people by name and see whether they're in the menu, in a raid or offline.
- You can chat from the menu, or press <b>Esc</b> during a raid for a quick chat.
- Chats show unread counts and "seen", and a little popup appears when someone messages you.
- You can block people. They can't message you, and you won't see them in game chat.

# Update [2026-09-24] Shop and locker
- You earn <b>gemdust</b> for banking gems, getting knockouts and finishing a raid in the top 3.
- The shop changes every day at midnight UTC, with 2 big items and 4 daily ones.
- There are 13 name styles (from a custom colour up to an animated rainbow) and 12 tank skins.
- You can send gifts to friends, and you get 3 refunds in total if you regret buying something.
- Equip things from your locker and they'll show up the next time you spawn.

# Update [2026-09-24] Ranked
- Ranks go from Bronze up to Emerald, and then Legend, where you get an actual number.
- Your first 3 games are placements, and they decide where you start.
- Every death shows how much rank you gained, and ranking up gets its own big screen.
- Rank badges now show next to names and on the leaderboard.
- There are daily quests for extra gemdust, and 12 medals to collect.
- There's a Top 100 leaderboard, and profiles that show your stats.

# Update [2026-09-24] Accounts
- You can log in with Discord or a name and password, or just keep playing as a guest. That's fine too.
- Your rank, gemdust and everything else get saved.
- You get a backup code when you sign up, in case you forget your password.
- In Account you can rename yourself every 14 days, link or unlink Discord, or delete your account.

# Balance [2026-09-24] Shop is cheaper
- Everything is about 15 to 20% cheaper, and the custom colour is now 8 gemdust.

# Patch [2026-09-24] Safety fixes
- I fixed some security holes on the server, and names are safer now.
- The menu starts in light mode now. The moon button switches it back.

# Patch [2026-09-23] Runs better
- You'll get a lot more FPS on laptops and Chromebooks, especially ones with weak graphics.
- The game lowers its own resolution if your computer is struggling.
- I fixed ping randomly jumping up to several seconds on bad wifi.
- If you lose connection, you rejoin automatically and land back in your raid.

# Update [2026-09-22] Shops, bosses and raid events
- Every raid has shop pads now, where you can spend gems on drills, gear and kit items like the Rock Barrage.
- Bosses show up, and each one is sitting on a huge pile of gems.
- There are loot chests, ore blooms and random raid events to find.
- Raid twists change the rules every few minutes.
- Bots are a lot smarter, and some of them almost feel like real people.

# Update [2026-09-18] Revenge

# Update [2026-09-16] Battle royale raids
- The game is now one big 2 hour battle royale raid that never stops, and you can join whenever you like.
- A storm closes in over time, so the fights come to you.
- Raids have scores, top spots and rewards at the end.
- You're protected after spawning until you move or shoot.
